A few clear answers

Before you follow along.

Can I download or try Greeter now?

Not yet. Greeter is in development. A seven-day trial with no card is planned, but trial activation and downloads are not available yet.

Are the $149 and $9 plans available to buy?

No. They are planned prices only. Checkout and final license terms are not ready.

What about OpenRouter costs?

A bring-your-own-key setup is planned. Model usage would be billed separately through your OpenRouter account. This website does not collect API keys.

Will Greeter be available for Windows?

Windows is planned after the macOS release. A release date is not set yet.
